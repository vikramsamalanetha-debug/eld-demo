Questions

What drivers ask us

Do I need an ELD?

Most CDL drivers who cross state lines and have to keep logs do. You may not need one if you stay within 150 air miles of your home terminal and work 14 hours or less, need logs 8 days or fewer in any 30 days, do driveaway-towaway work, or drive a truck with an engine older than model year 2000.

I only drive inside Tennessee. Do I still need one?

Rules for carriers that never leave the state are set by the state and can differ from the federal ones. Call us with your situation and we'll help you work out what applies to you.

What do I do when an officer asks for my logs?

Tap Inspection Mode. The officer sees today and the last 7 days on your screen, and you can send the official file by web services, email, Bluetooth or USB, whichever they ask for.

What happens if the device stops working?

The app shows a malfunction code. Switch to paper logs, tell your carrier, and the device has to be fixed or replaced within 8 days. We ship a replacement the next business day.

Can I fix a mistake in my log?

Yes. You can edit your status and add a note explaining why. Driving time recorded by the engine can't be deleted, and the original record is always kept. If the office suggests an edit, you approve or reject it.

Can I use the truck for personal errands after work?

Yes. Set your status to Personal Conveyance before you drive. That time isn't counted as driving, as long as you're truly off duty and not moving a load.

Does it work where there's no cell signal?

Yes. The device keeps recording with no signal. Logs sync to the office as soon as the phone reconnects.

Does it work for team drivers?

Yes. Both drivers log in on the same device and each keeps a separate log. Switching the active driver takes one tap.
